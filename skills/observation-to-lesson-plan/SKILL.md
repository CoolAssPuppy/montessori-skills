# Choose one response from evidence

Match what was seen to the school's album. Prefer an environment tweak or more practice when that fits.

## When to use

The guide has observations and wants a next teaching or environmental move.

Clean notes first with `factual-observation-notes`. Once a lesson is named, prepare it with `montessori-lesson-preparation`. Access changes: `inclusive-lesson-adaptations`.

## Inputs

- Observations (source-dated)
- The school's approved curriculum or album for this program
- Known prerequisites the guide has already presented
- Access needs and support plan already on file
- Constraints: time, other children, materials present

## Procedure

1. Split **happened** from **hypothesis**. "Built the pink tower twice, left it on the rug" vs "needs a grace-and-courtesy lesson on putting away."
2. Search the supplied curriculum for an objective that actually matches. Copy its ID. If none matches, say so. Do not mint a new official ID.
3. Check prerequisites listed in that curriculum. Missing prerequisite becomes the candidate, not the advanced lesson.
4. Consider, in this order: restore a complete material; change placement or crowding; offer more time with the same work; then a new presentation. Montessori, *Method* ch. VI: wait for attention, keep the lesson short.
5. For K-3 literacy or preschool/K math, you may note IES practice-guide themes (academic language and decoding; number progressions) as background. They do not replace the school's album or justify toddler drills. Full recommendation PDFs were not read for this package.
6. Choose **one** response. State what new observation would support keeping or dropping it. The guide reviews before it is taught.

Arevalo: observation, reflection, colleagues, family partnership. Do not convert every note into remediation. Do not infer mastery from exposure.

## Output

| Observation (fact) | Hypothesis (labeled) | Candidate from supplied curriculum (ID) | Prerequisites | Adaptation | Next observation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Plus: why an environment change was preferred or rejected.

## Adaptations

Keep the objective visible through an accessible response mode. If the album assumes standing work or handwriting, list that as a barrier and hand it to inclusive-lesson-adaptations rather than skipping the objective.

## Safeguards

No diagnosis. No guaranteed age of mastery. No copying proprietary album text into the output; cite lesson name/ID only.
