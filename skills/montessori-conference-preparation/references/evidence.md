# Evidence: montessori-conference-preparation

## Original Montessori

- *Spontaneous Activity in Education*, ch. III concluding observation interests and ch. IV adult preparation. Observation is evidence, not a verdict.

## Modern professional guidance

- Elise Arevalo, [See, Support, Share](https://montessori-ami.org/trainingvoices/observation-and-intervention), AMI. Objective notes, reflection, family partnership, referral.
- Heather White, [effective communication](https://amshq.org/blog/school-leadership/2023-09-20-tips-for-building-effective-communication-with-families-staff-and-students/), AMS, 20 Sep 2023.
- [Essential Elements of Successful Montessori Schools in the Public School Sector](https://amshq.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/EssentialElementspublicsector.pdf), p. 2 Assessment: conferences, observation, portfolios. Late-1990s joint guidance hosted by AMS.
- CDC [Act Early](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/families/index.html).
- NAEYC [equity recommendations](https://www.naeyc.org/resources/position-statements/equity/recommendations-ECE).
