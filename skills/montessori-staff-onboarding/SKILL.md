# Orient a new staff member

Pair the school's procedures with observation and supervised practice for this role.

## When to use

Someone new is joining as guide, assistant, specialist, or office staff.

One-day cover: `substitute-guide-handoff`. Parent sessions: `montessori-parent-workshops`.

## Inputs

- Role title and actual responsibilities
- Credentials already held
- Program (0-3, 3-6, 6-12, adolescent, office)
- Approved handbook, observation policy, record policy
- Named supervisor
- Access the role is allowed on day 1

## Procedure

1. Separate **already credentialed** from **local orientation**. A6 Montessori Teachers: orientation, in-service, consultation. Do not promise AMI/AMS credentials this school cannot grant.
2. CDC Quality Training Standards (1 Oct 2026) as a design frame, not a CDC badge: start from the role's tasks, write observable objectives, use accurate school content, practice, feedback, access, evaluation aligned to those objectives, follow-up (§§1-5, 7-8). Skip clinical public-health competencies.
3. Sequence: read the pages that apply; watch a work period without interrupting; rehearse one routine (snack, greeting, record entry) with a supervisor; debrief.
4. Teach work periods, respectful assistance, accessible communication, records, and escalation from **this** school's policies. *Spontaneous Activity* ch. IV: the adult learns to wait and to revise.
5. White: let staff reflect; rehearse sensitive family talk.
6. Check understanding with role-relevant scenarios, not a quiz about Maria Montessori's biography.
7. Public onboarding material uses synthetic children only.

A6 staffing remarks are late-1990s public-school recommendations, not a ratio law.

## Output

- Role plan with dates
- Reading list (school documents)
- Observation blocks
- Supervised practice
- Contacts
- Review checkpoints
- Access/permissions (what this role may not do yet)

## Adaptations

If the hire is office staff, skip album presentations and keep records, families, and calendar. If adolescent program, include supervision of practical work the school actually runs.

## Safeguards

No expanded FN roles. No real child examples in public files.
