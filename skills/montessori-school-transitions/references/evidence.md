# Evidence: montessori-school-transitions

## Original Montessori

- Indirect: *Own Handbook* "Freedom" (available adult) and *The Montessori Method* ch. VI (observe response). These skills do not supply a first-day protocol.

## Modern professional guidance

- Sara B. Tours and Lindsay R. Dennis, [Easing First Day Jitters](https://www.naeyc.org/resources/pubs/yc/sep2015/easing-first-day-jitters), NAEYC *Young Children*, Sep 2015. Basic, visual, and auditory home-to-school strategies. Preschool article; adapt for older learners. Hugging and eye contact optional. Magnets, laminating, photos, and recordings need safety and privacy checks.
- Heather White, [Tips for Building Effective Communication](https://amshq.org/blog/school-leadership/2023-09-20-tips-for-building-effective-communication-with-families-staff-and-students/), AMS, 20 Sep 2023. Share family/teacher goals from the start.
- NAEYC [equity recommendations](https://www.naeyc.org/resources/position-statements/equity/recommendations-ECE). Home language and access.

## Hapa Family (practitioner / parent)

- [Managing Transitions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CeZ-e-0zxKI). Predictable sequence, advance notice, optional visual supports.

## Editorial adaptations

- Named staff + pickup verification + review conversation: this skill's operational frame.
- No promised adjustment window.
- Peer buddy cannot replace adult duty of care (editorial, consistent with S7's monitoring note).
