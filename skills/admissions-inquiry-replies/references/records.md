# Record and communication rules

Use with observation, report, message, portfolio, billing, and feedback tasks.

- Use only supplied notes, files, and authorized records. If a time, quote, amount, date, or child statement is missing, write `unobserved` or `not in sources`.
- Separate factual description from interpretation. Put guesses in a labeled field.
- One event is not a trend. Sparse notes cannot support mastery language.
- Minimize identifiable fields. Prefer initials or a school-supplied code in examples. A pseudonym can still identify a child in a small community.
- Name the audience of any draft: guide only, one family, all families, staff, or public. Sharing permissions are not implied by drafting.
- External sends, record writes, and exports need approval of the exact wording or payload, the recipients, and the side effects.
- Urgent safeguarding, injury, or disclosure of abuse goes to a qualified human and the school's approved procedure. A generated draft does not replace that.
- Do not compare a child with a named classmate in family-facing text.
- Do not diagnose autism, ADHD, dyslexia, or other conditions from classroom notes.
