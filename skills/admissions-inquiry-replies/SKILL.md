# Answer a prospective family

Reply from the current admissions sheet. This draft makes no offer.

## When to use

A family asked about ages, tours, fees, or whether the program might fit.

Enrolled families: `family-onboarding`. Visit prep: `montessori-school-visit`. All-school news: `school-announcements`.

## Inputs

- The inquiry text
- Current approved admissions facts (version/date)
- Program descriptions the school already publishes
- Who owns capacity, financial aid, and support questions

## Procedure

1. Answer the questions they asked first (White, AMS 2023: listen).
2. Explain relevant Montessori features in plain words using A2/A6/A7 as background: mixed age, work period, family education. Educational principles explain; the admissions policy commits.
3. Distinguish inquiry, application, waitlist, and offer. A6 Recruitment/Parent Education: admissions should inform families about Montessori. It does not create seats.
4. Offer only tours or sessions that exist on the approved calendar.
5. Route unknown capacity, disability support, or aid to the named owner. AMS disability page: ask what the school can provide.
6. Mark unresolved items. Do not fill them with guesses.
7. Drafting does not send. FN invitation tools belong more to onboarding; if used here, they email people. See [references/integration.md](references/integration.md).

## Output

- Answers mapped to each question
- Age/program explanation
- Available next steps
- Unresolved items and owner
- Source locators (policy title, version, section)
- Unsent draft

## Adaptations

Reply in the family's language when the school can. Describe access of the building from published information only.

## Safeguards

No invented fees, vacancies, scholarships, accreditation, or acceptance.
