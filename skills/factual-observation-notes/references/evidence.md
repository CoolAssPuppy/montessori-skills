# Evidence: factual-observation-notes

## Original Montessori

- Maria Montessori, *Spontaneous Activity in Education*, 1917, ch. III concluding observation guide (selected activity, duration, repetition, persistence, interruption, peers); ch. IV, preparation of the teacher. [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/24727). The chapter III example concerns a child around 3. Historical interference with concentration is not a recommended test.

## Modern professional guidance

- Elise Arevalo, [See, Support, Share](https://montessori-ami.org/trainingvoices/observation-and-intervention), AMI.
- NAEYC [equity recommendations](https://www.naeyc.org/resources/position-statements/equity/recommendations-ECE): observer bias, strengths, specialist collaboration.
- GDPR Article 5 principles (purpose, minimization, accuracy) as a reminder to minimize identifiable data in drafts: [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng). Local law needs local review.
