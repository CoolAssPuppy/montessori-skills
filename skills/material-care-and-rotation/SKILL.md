# Care for materials and justify what is out

Keep work complete and safe. Change availability for a reason you can write down.

## When to use

Something is broken, incomplete, unused, overcrowded, or newly needed.

Whole-room layout: `classroom-environment-review`. Lesson sequence: `montessori-lesson-preparation`.

## Inputs

- Material name and album ID if the school uses one
- Condition photos or a walk-through list
- Cleaning instructions from manufacturer or school
- Observation of recent use, if any
- Whether this is a formal sequence (classroom) or a home selection

## Procedure

1. Inventory: pieces present, damaged, missing, soiled.
2. Remove an immediate hazard (broken glass, mold, recalled item, magnet/battery loose).
3. Follow written cleaning directions. Do not invent a disinfection protocol.
4. Formal classroom sequence: missing control pieces pause the presentation. Repair or replace before presenting. *Method* ch. XX: sequence and error control vary by material.
5. Availability change: write the reason (incomplete; unsafe; child using it elsewhere; crowding; seasonal). No inspected primary text sets a weekly rotation rule. Do not pull a child's ongoing work to "freshen" a shelf.
6. Unused material does not mean unreadiness. Note last observed use, then watch, ask the child, or wait. Hapa Family (H4) notes children may dislike particular materials; dislike alone does not justify discarding a classroom sequence.
7. Set a review date. DIY replacements need the same safety checks as purchased ones.

Home selections may rotate more freely than a school sequence; still keep safety and a way to bring work back.

## Output

- Checklist (complete / missing / unsafe)
- Repair, clean, or replenish action
- Proposed availability log: in / out / reason / review date / who decided
- Link to album ID if supplied

## Adaptations

Store a frequently used adapted piece with the material, labeled, so a substitute can find it.

## Safeguards

No unsafe DIY for choking, magnets, or chemicals. Product sanitation needs actual instructions.
