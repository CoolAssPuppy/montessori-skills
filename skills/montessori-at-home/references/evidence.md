# Evidence: montessori-at-home

## Original Montessori

- Maria Montessori, *The Montessori Method*, 1912 English edition, tr. Anne E. George. [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/39863). Ch. V, independence and limits; ch. VI, short individual presentations; ch. VII, practical life. The ch. VII timetable is explicitly a proposal that was not followed in full.
- Maria Montessori, *Dr. Montessori's Own Handbook*, 1914. [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/29635). Section "Freedom": calm guidance, responsive help, companionship.
- Maria Montessori, *To Educate the Human Potential*, Kalakshetra 1955 impression, introduction and ch. I-II. [Internet Archive text](https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.136487/2015.136487.To-Educate-The-Human-Potential-Ed-2nd_djvu.txt). Elementary work includes reasons, group life, and imagination connected to study. Do not treat universal-age or exclusionary claims as eligibility rules.
- Adolescent social and practical independence: Montessori quoted by AMI, [Montessori 12-18](https://montessori-ami.org/resource-library/quotes/montessori-12-18), excerpts from *From Childhood to Adolescence* pp. 65-75. Land-based earning is a historical proposal, not a required home program.

## Modern professional guidance

- American Montessori Society, [5 Core Components](https://amshq.org/the-ams-difference/core-components-of-montessori/). Classroom work-period lengths are AMS program recommendations, not a home timetable.
- CDC, [Learn the Signs. Act Early.](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/families/index.html), updated 6 May 2026. Milestone monitoring and clinician conversation when a family has concerns.

## Hapa Family (practitioner / parent)

- [Montessori at home with older children (6-12)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H2WTBBameXw). Whole-home access and household responsibility. The speaker describes a child around 5 approaching 6; treat full 6-12 claims as interpretation.

## Editorial adaptations

- One interest, one change, one activity, one review question: task design for this skill, not a Montessori instrument.

- No efficacy claim for this starting plan. Program-level research (Randolph et al. 2023; León, Lipnevich, and García-Garrido 2026 abstract) does not validate a home activity. The 2026 full article was not read.
