# Turn a handbook section into a checklist

Extract what staff must do. Leave the policy text unchanged.

## When to use

Staff need an operational list for late pickup, illness, visitors, or another approved section.

Family-facing notice of a change: `school-announcements`. New staff reading: `montessori-staff-onboarding`.

## Inputs

- Handbook file, version, date, pages
- Program it applies to
- Policy owner
- Staff audience
- Any related form

## Procedure

1. Record title, version, page, owner.
2. Copy required actions and exceptions in the handbook's order. Quote the clause that supports each step.
3. Label Montessori explanatory sentences as **context** and enforceable lines as **rule**.
4. Mark contradictions or missing roles for the owner. Do not silently pick a winner.
5. Add trigger, responsible role, escalation, and a realistic test scenario.
6. A6 Administration: continuity; A5 staff: clarity. Montessori *Spontaneous Activity* ch. IV is adult preparation as a principle, not a legal method.
7. Flag an obvious safety gap for qualified review while still reporting what the policy currently says.

Full jurisdictional analysis is out of scope.

## Output

- Versioned procedure
- Source locators
- Role, trigger, steps, exceptions, escalation
- Owner-review questions
- Scenario test
- What was not changed

## Adaptations

If programs differ (0-3 vs elementary), keep separate procedures rather than averaging them.

## Safeguards

No invented sanctions, deadlines, or statutes.
