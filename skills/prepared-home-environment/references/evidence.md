# Evidence: prepared-home-environment

## Original Montessori

- Maria Montessori, *Spontaneous Activity in Education* (Advanced Montessori Method, vol. I), 1917, tr. Florence Simmonds. [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/24727). Ch. V, environment proportioned to reach and strength; purposeful movement. Fragile crockery appears as feedback in the wider work; it is not a modern injury-prevention standard.
- Maria Montessori, *Dr. Montessori's Own Handbook*, 1914. [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/29635). "Motor Education," pp. 20-29: dressing frames and everyday care tools.

## Modern professional guidance

- CDC, [Choking Hazards](https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/foods-and-drinks/choking-hazards.html), 11 Mar 2026.
- CDC, [Providing Care for Babies to Sleep Safely](https://www.cdc.gov/sudden-infant-death/sleep-safely/index.html), 17 Sep 2024, reflecting AAP 2022.
- CPSC, [Small Parts and Choking Hazard Labeling FAQs](https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Small-Parts-and-Choking-Hazard-Labeling-FAQs).
- CPSC, [Anchor It!](https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/Video/Anchor-It-How-to-Anchor-It-and-protect-children-from-furniture-tip-overs), 2 Feb 2022. Explanatory page read; video not viewed.

## Hapa Family (practitioner / parent)

- [5 Lessons Learned After 5 Years of Montessori Parenting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eiQhQi5ABCA). Use existing tools; DIY and secondhand still need safety checks.
- [Montessori at home with older children (6-12)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H2WTBBameXw). Organize access across the home. Appliance, pet, and outdoor independence need their own current safety rules.

## Editorial adaptations

- Hazard-first ordering (anchor, chemicals, sleep, choking, then access) is this skill's sequence. Montessori texts do not supply CPSC or CDC rules.
- Low-cost / already-owned preference is an editorial choice aligned with Hapa's existing-tools advice, not a ban on purchases a family freely makes.
- AMS classroom prepared-environment language describes schools. It does not set a home aesthetic standard.
