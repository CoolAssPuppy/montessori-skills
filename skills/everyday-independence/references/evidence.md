# Evidence: everyday-independence

## Original Montessori

- Maria Montessori, *The Montessori Method*, 1912. Ch. V, independence taught with patience; ch. VI, concise presentation; ch. VII, practical life. [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/39863).
- Maria Montessori, *Dr. Montessori's Own Handbook*, 1914. "Motor Education," dressing-frame demonstration and everyday care. [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/29635).

## Modern professional guidance

- CDC choking guidance for any food-preparation task: [Choking Hazards](https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/foods-and-drinks/choking-hazards.html), 11 Mar 2026.
- CPSC [small parts FAQs](https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Small-Parts-and-Choking-Hazard-Labeling-FAQs) when tools or toys can break.
- NAEYC equity recommendations: meaningful scaffolds and home language, [Recommendations for Early Childhood Educators](https://www.naeyc.org/resources/position-statements/equity/recommendations-ECE).

## Hapa Family (practitioner / parent)

- [Guiding Cooperation with Routines + Cleaning Up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaaQYHbZgZ4). Scaffold dressing; allow time; reduce help gradually; remain available. Do not copy the discussed soup-left-out example; clean food promptly.
- [Montessori at home with older children (6-12)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H2WTBBameXw). Adapt the real household job rather than toddler shelf work.

## Editorial adaptations

- Assistance ladder (wait, cue, stabilize, do the hard sub-step) is this skill's wording. It is compatible with Montessori's available adult in "Freedom," not a published Montessori protocol.
- Observation prompt fields are adapted from the spirit of *Spontaneous Activity* ch. III's observation interests (choice, duration, repetition, interruption). They are not Montessori's instrument and are not a diagnostic test.
