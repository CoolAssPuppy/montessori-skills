- *The Montessori Method*, 1912, ch. V (discipline/independence), VI (presentation), XII and XX (sensorial materials and control of error). [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/39863).
- *Own Handbook*, 1914, "Freedom."
- AMI, [Four Planes of Development](https://montessori-ami.org/resource-library/quotes/four-planes-development), selected quotations (not full books).
- *To Educate the Human Potential*, ch. I-II (cosmic/elementary shift).
- AMI, [Montessori 12-18](https://montessori-ami.org/resource-library/quotes/montessori-12-18) for adolescent vocabulary in excerpts.
