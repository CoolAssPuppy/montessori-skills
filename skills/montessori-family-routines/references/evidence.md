# Evidence: montessori-family-routines

## Original Montessori

- Maria Montessori, *The Montessori Method*, 1912, ch. VII. Proposed timetable; the text says it was never followed entirely. [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/39863).

## Modern professional guidance

- AMS [uninterrupted work periods](https://amshq.org/the-ams-difference/core-components-of-montessori/) apply to programs, not homes.
- NAEYC [home-to-school transition strategies](https://www.naeyc.org/resources/pubs/yc/sep2015/easing-first-day-jitters) support predictable sequences and optional visual/auditory cues. Hugging and eye contact remain optional.

## Hapa Family (practitioner / parent)

- [Guiding Cooperation with Routines + Cleaning Up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaaQYHbZgZ4). Advance choices; unsuccessful charts as information; remain available. Do not reproduce food spoilage as a natural consequence.
- [Managing Transitions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CeZ-e-0zxKI). Predictable sequence rather than a rigid clock; advance notice; individual response to timers. Eye contact is not a prerequisite. Physical follow-through in the video is not permission for restraint.

- Bounded choice plus fallback completion is this skill's pattern.
