# Summarize family feedback

Keep minority views. Do not turn comments into a fake score.

## When to use

The school has survey comments, meeting notes, or a comment box to review.

A reply to one family: `family-school-messages`. Workshop follow-up: `montessori-parent-workshops`.

## Inputs

- Question asked
- Collection period
- Number invited and number of responses if known
- The comments themselves
- Whether responses were anonymous

## Procedure

1. State the denominator, e.g. 18 of 40 families. A mood label without a count cannot support a theme claim.
2. Group comments while keeping their meaning. White: listen to understand; let staff reflect. S6: culturally aware partnership.
3. Keep contrary and minority views in the table, not in a footnote you skip.
4. Separate requests ("more outdoor time") from reported events ("pickup was 20 minutes late on 3 Oct"). Route urgent safeguarding out of this summary into the human procedure.
5. S8 §6 applies only if this is workshop/training evaluation. Do not treat a satisfaction item as proof the school "works."
6. Action candidates stay labeled **candidate**. Feedback does not authorize a reply or a policy change.

Do not invent sentiment scores. Do not deanonymize.

## Output

| Theme | Count / denominator | Example (de-identified) | Contrary views | Evidence notes | Action candidate | Owner question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Adaptations

If comments mix languages, do not translate meaning you are unsure of; mark for a bilingual staff member.
