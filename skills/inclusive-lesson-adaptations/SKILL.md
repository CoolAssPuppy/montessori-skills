# Adapt a lesson without dropping the objective

Change the barrier. Keep the concept the album names. Follow the child's plan.

## When to use

A named lesson will fail because of material, position, language, timing, or sensory load, and the team wants a usable version.

Unmodified prep: `montessori-lesson-preparation`. Next-lesson choice: `observation-to-lesson-plan`.

## Inputs

- Lesson and objective from the supplied album
- Known access needs, home language, devices, and the individual plan
- Strengths the team has already observed
- Specialist guidance already in the file
- Safety requirements that cannot move

## Procedure

1. Write the objective in one line from the album. If you cannot see it after the adaptation, the adaptation is too loose.
2. Name the barrier: task, material, communication, environment, or timing. AMS disability guidance: individualized instruction, accommodations, family partnership, and what the school can actually provide. It does not claim Montessori treats ADHD, autism, or dyslexia.
3. List learner strengths and preferred response modes (pointing, speech, sign, device, matching, building).
4. Change one barrier at a time where possible: table height, tray lip, larger contrast, extra time, preview in the home language, seated presentation, adult stabilizing the object.
5. Check whether the control of error or the concept is still observable (*Method* ch. VI: watch the response).
6. Coordinate with specialists and family knowledge. Do not override an individualized plan or an essential safety rule.
7. Write the observation check: what will show the concept, in this mode.

NAEYC: home language, disability inclusion, meaningful scaffolds. Eye contact, silence, handwriting, and unsupported independence are not universal prerequisites.

## Output

| Objective | Barrier | Adaptation | Strengths used | Response mode | Support | Observation check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Adaptations

If the school cannot provide a service, say so and name who can answer. Do not promise a placement.

## Safeguards

No diagnosis, no treatment claims, no "this child cannot." Safety requirements stay.
