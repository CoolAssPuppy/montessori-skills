# Hand off the room without improvising

Give a temporary guide what they must know. Leave specialist presentations for the regular guide.

## When to use

A trained or school-approved substitute will cover a defined time.

Ordinary planning: `mixed-age-work-cycle`. New hire orientation: `montessori-staff-onboarding`.

## Inputs

- Coverage hours
- School-approved handoff template if any
- Current work already in progress (from notes)
- Adult roles that day
- Where plans, keys, and first-aid live
- Authorized health/access instructions through the school's channel
- Emergency and escalation contacts

## Procedure

1. Use current school documents. A6: teacher orientation and administrative continuity. If a fact is stale, mark the time it was true.
2. Need-to-know only: who is in charge, the day's sequence, snack/rest, outdoor rules, pickup.
3. Current work: names of materials children are repeating, not new album presentations the substitute has not been trained to give.
4. Access and safety: only authorized instructions, stored where the school requires. Do not paste a full family history.
5. Split **routine questions** (ask the assistant) from **escalate now** (office, nurse, designated safeguarding lead).
6. Tell the substitute what to leave in writing for the returning guide.
7. Confirm contacts still work.

*Spontaneous Activity* ch. IV-V: the adult prepares and the environment should already be usable. A handoff is not Montessori certification and does not expand FN permissions.

## Output

- Contacts
- Timetable
- Current work to protect
- Adult roles
- Access/safety pointers (channel, not extra copies of records)
- Escalation
- Version/date
- What not to present

## Adaptations

If the substitute does not share a language with a family, say who translates.

See [references/records.md](references/records.md). No invented meds or emergency steps. No extra system access.
