# Draft a progress report from evidence

Write only what the period's records support. Leave blanks visible.

## When to use

A reporting period is due and the guide has observations, work, and a rubric.

Raw notes: `factual-observation-notes`. Sample selection: `learning-portfolios`. Family conference agenda: `montessori-conference-preparation`.

## Inputs

- Reporting period and audience
- Observations and work with dates
- School rubric and status definitions
- Access / support already documented
- Family language for the published version

## Procedure

1. Name period, audience, and every source file. A6 Assessment lists observation, portfolios, performance/rubrics, conferences.
2. Group examples by the school's learning areas. One instance stays "seen once." Change over time needs more than one dated example.
3. Describe assistance and access in the same sentence as the work: "with a modeled first step," "using AAC," "independently after the presentation on 4 Mar (notes)."
4. State missing evidence instead of smoothing. Positive tone cannot replace a blank.
5. Invite family perspective as a question, not as a paragraph you invented for them.
6. The guide verifies every substantive sentence before sharing.
7. If FN `progress_assessment_create` is requested, read [references/integration.md](references/integration.md). `shared_with_parent: true` notifies guardians. Draft first.

Arevalo and White: partnership, listening, no motive-reading. D1: minimize identifiers in examples. Do not compare named peers. Do not diagnose.

## Output

- Strengths with evidence locators
- Progress where dated examples show change
- Support in place
- Next steps from the supplied curriculum
- Missing evidence
- Reviewer checkbox for the guide

## Adaptations

Translate the family-facing version. Keep technical IDs in the staff copy.

## Safeguards

See [references/records.md](references/records.md). Sparse notes cannot support mastery language.

## Optional FN

Standalone draft first. Sharing in FN is a notification event.
