## Maria Montessori

**M1.** *The Montessori Method* (1912 English, tr. Anne E. George). [Gutenberg 39863](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/39863). Read: ch. V, VI, VII, X, XIV, XIX, XX; selected XII and XVII. Holmes's introduction is not Montessori.

**M2.** *Spontaneous Activity in Education* (1917, Advanced Method vol. I, tr. Florence Simmonds). [Gutenberg 24727](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/24727). Read: ch. IV, V; parts of III and VI.

**M3.** *Dr. Montessori's Own Handbook* (1914). [Gutenberg 29635](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/29635). Read: Motor Education, Freedom, Moral Factors.

Early texts include ableist labels, coercive examples, and obsolete medical claims. Those are not procedures.

## Empirical studies

These studies describe Montessori *programs*, not this skill package. Skills do not claim that following a procedure will reproduce a pooled effect. The two reviews disagree in how they read the same literature; that disagreement is recorded here, not settled in the README.

**E1.** Randolph et al. 2023 Campbell review. [Summary](https://www.campbellcollaboration.org/review/montessori-impact-on-academic-and-nonacademic-outcomes/). Full methods not independently audited. Program-level effects; not a skill validation. Do not repeat the summary's country-count inconsistency.

**E2.** León, Lipnevich, and García-Garrido 2026, *Review of Educational Research*. DOI [10.3102/00346543261458396](https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543261458396). Crossref JSON confirmed title, authors, journal, date, abstract. Publisher full text not read (timeout/403 on automated GET 8 Nov 2026). Abstract reports nominally positive pooled effects with heterogeneity, small-study effects, and weak transparency.

**E3.** Courtier et al. 2021. [PMC8518750](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8518750/). Public-school RCT with limits (implementation, exclusions, assessor knowledge).

**E4, E5.** IES/WWC reading and early math practice-guide landing pages. Full PDFs not read.
